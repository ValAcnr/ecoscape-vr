# Outreach email templates

Replace the `[brackets]`. Keep it short: the goal is a 15-minute call or a one-line letter of interest, not a contract.

---

## English

**Subject:** Student project on urban planting — 15 minutes of your advice?

Dear [First name / Team],

I'm Valentin, a business-school student working with three classmates on **EcoScape**, a concept we'll pitch in December 2026. Players learn to choose, water and care for plants through a short game on their phone or in VR. When they succeed, they unlock a **real planting** in their neighbourhood, carried out by a local partner (up to four per player).

We admire [specific thing: e.g. "your Citizen Pruner training" / "your free tree giveaways"], and we think [organisation] could be an ideal [planting partner / sponsor / data partner].

Would you have **15 minutes** in the next two weeks to tell us whether this would be useful to you and what we might be missing? A short reply on your interest in principle would also help us a lot.

Prototype: https://valacnr.github.io/ecoscape-vr/

Thank you for your time,
Valentin [Last name]
[School] — Technology & Partnerships Lead, EcoScape
[Email] · [Phone]
